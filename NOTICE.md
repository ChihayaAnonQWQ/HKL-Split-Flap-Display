## 版面设计的出处 / Origin of the layout design

本资源包的**版面设计**复刻自 **MTR 官方服务器（MTR Let's Play）** 上由 **Forest_E233**
修建的**花越线（Hana-Koshi Line）** 站台的翻牌显示器。

The **visual layout** of this resource pack is a recreation of the split-flap display on the
**Hana-Koshi Line (花越线)** platforms, built by **Forest_E233** on the
**official MTR server (MTR Let's Play)**.

- 服务器介绍 / Server wiki: <https://wiki.minecrafttransitrailway.com/zh:mtr_letsplay:start>
- 原版比例 3:2，Joban PIDS 画布 2:1，因此版面有细微差别
  (original is 3:2, Joban's PIDS canvas is 2:1, hence the small layout differences)

该设计仅供**学习与个人使用**，版权归原作者所有。

The design is included for **study and personal use**; all rights remain with its original author.
